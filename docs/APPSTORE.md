## Description
**See harmony. Hear it. Play it.**

The Harmony Compass lays out every key, its relative minor, the
dominants that pull on them, and the diminished families that bind it
all — as one navigable wheel. Tap a chord and it sounds; its legal
moves light up and explain themselves in a player's words: resolve,
deceptive, ii–V, tritone sub, backdoor, the dominant chain, passing
diminished, Barry Harris movement.

**Every pathway opens into your hands.**
- Scales matched to each chord — 20 of them, from Ionian to altered
  and the symmetric colors — spelled correctly, drawn on a rosewood
  fretboard with guide-tone highlighting, playable at a tap.
- Grips the way charts print them: open classics, barres, shells,
  drop-2 in every inversion, triads per string set — never a shape no
  hand plays.
- Voice leading you can watch: tap a resolution and the voices travel
  on a staff — half-step pulls in green, common tones held — while the
  chords sound.
- Alterations with judgment: every tension a chord carries, when it's
  the right call, and the scale that covers it.
- Movement, Barry Harris style: the 6th-diminished ladder that turns a
  parked chord into motion, rung by rung.

**Honest theory.** Every musical fact in Fretspace is pinned by tests
against the standard literature — spellings, resolutions, voicings,
scales. If it's on screen, it's correct.

Built native for Apple Vision Pro. iOS and web companions on the way —
one engine, one truth, everywhere you practice.
